Art and the City

Art and the City is a multi-page net-art website exploring how urban environments shape creativity, culture, and personal identity. Through music, travel storytelling, and original artwork, the site presents cities as living canvases that influence how we create, move, and express ourselves. Designed and built entirely from scratch, this project blends visual storytelling with front-end development fundamentals.

Role: UX/UI Designer & Front-End Designer Timeline: 2 weeks Tools: HTML / CSS -- Adobe / Github / VSC

Problem

Creative culture is often experienced in fragments—music on one platform, art on another, and personal storytelling somewhere else entirely. This separation makes it difficult to understand how place, culture, and creativity intersect and influence one another.

Solution

I designed a cohesive, multi-page web experience that brings music, art, and storytelling into one digital space. Through consistent navigation, bold visuals, and interactive elements, the site connects these creative expressions into a unified narrative centered on city life.

Process

I began by defining the core concept: the city as a creative catalyst. From there, I structured the site around three content pillars—Music, Blog, and Art—ensuring each section felt distinct while remaining visually connected. I designed the layout and visual system, built the pages using HTML and CSS, and layered in JavaScript to enhance interaction, navigation behavior, and usability. The project was refined through testing, iteration, and validation.

Deliverables

Fully responsive multi-page website hosted on GitHub Pages, UX/UI layouts, visual storytelling components, written content across all sections, JavaScript-enhanced interactions, and a Works Cited page emphasizing ethical sourcing and transparency.
