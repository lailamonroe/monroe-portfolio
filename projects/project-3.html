Advertising Campaign

poppi mini

poppi mini is a campaign introducing poppi’s prebiotic soda in a smaller, on-the-go can. The work highlights big flavor and real benefits in a playful, lifestyle-driven way designed to resonate with Gen Z and Millennial consumers.

Role: Designer Timeline: 3 weeks Tools: Figma / Adobe
Overview Process Deliverables Gallery

Problem

Many better-for-you beverages struggle to balance health messaging with fun, flavor, and cultural relevance. Younger consumers want healthier options but often feel that “healthy” drinks lack personality or feel restrictive.

poppi mini needed to launch as both functional and fun, proving that wellness does not have to come at the cost of enjoyment or aesthetics.

Solution

This campaign positions poppi mini as small but powerful. Bright visuals, playful copy, and bold layouts communicate convenience, flavor, and gut-health benefits at a glance.

The message stays simple and memorable: big flavor, real benefits, now in a mini can.

Process

I began by researching poppi’s existing brand voice and audience, focusing on how Gen Z and Millennials engage with health-forward products. This audience values wellness but also cares deeply about aesthetics, personality, and shareability.

From there, I translated the product benefits into visually led messaging that felt light, joyful, and lifestyle-driven rather than instructional. Concepts were refined through iterations on color, hierarchy, and tone to ensure the campaign felt bold, modern, and unmistakably poppi.

The final executions balance clarity and playfulness, allowing the mini format to feel intentional rather than reduced.

Deliverables

Print advertisements, a large-format billboard, social media ads, and audio spots, including 30-second and 60-second radio executions.
